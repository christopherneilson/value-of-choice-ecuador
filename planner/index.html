What the paper found

Two attributes carry a robust signal in every grade: a larger building footprint is worth about 0.4 km of extra travel to families, a more fragmented campus (higher building count) about 0.3 km less. The other eight are individually imprecise. And with all ten in the model — plus learned features from the photographs — most of the dispersion in school desirability remains unexplained. Desirability is real, large, and largely idiosyncratic; no parsimonious set of observable amenities substitutes for asking families. paper, real data
