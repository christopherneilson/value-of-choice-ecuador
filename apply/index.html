Two rules run on your application: the distance rule Manta used before the reform, and the deferred acceptance it adopted. The paper's whole argument, from where you are standing.

    The families you compete against are synthetic: drawn from the paper's estimated demand model over Manta's real schools, seats and cantons, with homes sampled from a coarse density grid. School desirability is shown as a quintile band only, and school names are not published here. Your own ranking is an order, not a scale, so this page reports which of your choices you were given and never a welfare number in kilometres. Deferred acceptance runs as it was deployed: your list first, then the remaining schools appended in distance order, with sibling priority above listed schools above appended ones and one lottery number per applicant and school. The distance rule assigns in distance order with one lottery number per applicant. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors.
