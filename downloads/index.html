The Value of Choice in Centralized School Assignment · downloads

Downloads and teaching kit

Everything on this site that can be reused: the synthetic Manta as JSON, the assignment engine as a standalone module, a notebook that rebuilds the paper's rules from scratch and checks them against the engine, and the paper itself. All of it is synthetic, derived or aggregated; nothing identifies a family. Please cite the paper when you use it.

The teaching notebook

Plain Python, standard library only, about a minute to run. It loads the site's data, writes applicant-proposing deferred acceptance in fifteen lines, reproduces the site engine's assignment for every one of the 1,098 entry-grade families under both rules, averages twenty lotteries to recover the simulator's numbers, then changes the market: takes 30% of the seats away, and asks what the value of choice would be if families only cared about distance. Four exercises at the end, from stable improvement cycles to running it on your own city.

applicants_g4.json		The synthetic families, one file per entry grade (Preschool 1, Preschool 2, Primary 1; 1,098 / 885 / 389 families). Per family: a synthetic home, its taste for distance, list length, number of schools considered, sibling school, the list it submitted, and its utility for every school. The published parameters of the demand model are in the file header.
home_density_cells.jsonThe 300 m density grid the homes are drawn from: cell centres and counts, cells with fewer than five households removed (187 cells).

survey_aggregates.jsonTabulations of the parent survey by list length and grade: no group below twenty respondents, no count between one and four (the survey explorer).

The engine

The rules the paper compares, as a dependency-free ES module you can import from any page or from Node: deferred acceptance, the distance rule, the stable-improvement-cycles benchmark, the market builder, the parameter rescaler and the seeded simulator. It is validated against the research pipeline's Python engines on one lottery draw per grade, family by family; the fixtures are published with it.
