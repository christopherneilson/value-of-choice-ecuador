The point

New York's 45.0% band bundles coordination, digitization and acting on preferences — none of the three can be separated out. Manta held the first two fixed and changed only the objective, so its 87.6% band is acting on preferences alone. Read the small green bands too: what a cleverer algorithm adds among coordinated mechanisms is 3.3% of the range in New York and 0.3% in Manta.
